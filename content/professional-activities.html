Profile

Academic and Research Profile

Academic Position

Since April 2026, I have been a professor in the School of Automation at Beijing Institute of Technology (北京理工大学自动化学院) and a member of the State Key Lab of Autonomous Intelligent Unmanned Systems (自主智能无人系统全国重点实验室).

Research Focus

My research spans genetic programming, reinforcement learning, dynamic scheduling, multi-objective optimization, and AI for intelligent manufacturing.

Industrial R&D

From July 2024 to March 2026, I worked as a Scientist at SIMTech, A*STAR, Singapore, developing AI-driven optimization methods for manufacturing.

Academic Collaboration

From October 2024 to March 2026, I held a joint postdoctoral appointment at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, under the supervision of Professor Yew-Soon Ong.

Open-Source Contributions

I release research code and benchmarks through GitHub repositories including Fog-Computing, InventoryControl, GPDFJSS, NichGPDRL, and GPDFJSS-MENG.
